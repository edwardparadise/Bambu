Why we started BAMBU Interiors

A London interiors business built around a direct relationship between the client, designer and delivery team.

An earlier conversation

The most useful time to meet a contractor can be before a lease is agreed. Access hours, existing services and the landlord’s approval process can all change what a floor will cost to fit out. We started BAMBU to bring those questions into the first conversation.

Who we work with

We work with occupiers considering a move, landlords preparing a floor for letting and operators testing a flexible workspace. Each starts with a different question. A tenant may need somewhere for confidential calls. An operator needs to know how much of the floor can earn its keep.

What to bring

A plan, a location and a rough date are enough to begin. We can identify which assumptions need testing before anyone commits to a specification.
