Launching the new BAMBU Interiors brand

BAMBU launched in 2026 to apply over 15 years of commercial interiors experience to a simpler way of working. Design and construction belong in the same conversation from day one.

A simple way to work

We start by walking the space with you. We agree what it needs to do, then test the layout with the budget, programme and building constraints in view. Once we have a direction, the same project lead stays involved through detailed design, construction and handover. You know who to call and where decisions stand.

Design that makes an impression

Knowing how something will be built gives us room to be more ambitious. In this reception concept, a sculpted desk and suspended globe lighting give visitors something memorable from the moment they arrive. The design, joinery and lighting are considered together so the idea can carry through into the finished space.

If you’re planning a move or refurbishment, we’d be happy to walk the space with you. Get in touch.
