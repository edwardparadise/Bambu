The essentials

What we look at.

Understand the building

Review available floor plans, base-build information, access arrangements and the existing services strategy. Identify where specialist surveys or further records are needed.

Test the brief

Consider occupancy, meeting rooms, circulation, reception, amenities and the practical relationship between spaces. Compare options rather than fixing a layout too early.

Understand cost and programme

Set out assumptions, potential long-lead items, enabling works, approvals and the key decisions that affect cost and timing.

Prepare for landlord discussions

Clarify potential alterations, consent requirements, landlord interfaces and the information that may be needed for a Licence to Alter.

Useful to know

Questions worth asking.

Is a test fit the same as a technical design?

No. A test fit explores whether the brief can work within the available area. Detailed technical design, surveys and approvals follow as the scheme develops.

Can you help before heads of terms are agreed?

Yes. Early feasibility can inform negotiations, but legal and commercial lease advice remains with your appointed advisers.
