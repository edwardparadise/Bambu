THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE BUILD

Specialists where they matter. One coordinated plan.

We bring in the trades the building needs and agree who owns each part of the work.

01 / PRINCIPLE

The right people for the actual scope.

Specialist trades may include mechanical and electrical contractors, partitions and glazing, joinery, flooring, decoration, furniture, AV, data and other packages. The mix depends on the building, the design and the agreed procurement approach.

02 / PRACTICE

Selection is more than a price comparison.

We consider relevant experience, technical capability, available resources, programme fit, insurances and applicable competence requirements. Product-specific installation requirements and building rules must also be understood.

03 / PRINCIPLE

Interfaces are where projects succeed or fail.

A glazed partition meets a ceiling. An AV installation needs power and data. A joinery package needs dimensions that work on site. We focus on responsibilities, sequencing, information and change control between packages.

04 / PRACTICE

Clear accountability, agreed contractually.

BAMBU's role and responsibility depend on the agreed appointment and procurement route. We explain who is contracted to do what, how specialist packages are coordinated and where approvals or third-party sign-off are required.

05 / PRINCIPLE

Quality that can be checked.

We look for documented specifications, appropriate samples, inspections, commissioning where relevant and an organised handover. We do not imply an unverified subcontractor certification or guarantee an outcome before the scope is agreed.

06 / PRACTICE

A network that can evolve.

New products and specialist techniques can be valuable. We remain open to suitable suppliers, while checking technical evidence, proven installation methods and long-term support before specifying them.
