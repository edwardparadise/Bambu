Plain-English reference

Know what the project language means.

Common UK fit-out terms and what they mean when you read a brief, cost plan or landlord’s guide.

Cat A

A common description for the base level of landlord-provided office finishes and services. The precise scope varies by building and agreement.

Cat B

A common description for the occupier-specific fit-out, which may include meeting rooms, finishes, furniture and technology.

Test fit

An early spatial study that checks whether an intended brief can be accommodated in a particular floor area.

Licence to Alter

The landlord's contractual consent process for proposed tenant alterations, where required.

M&E

Mechanical and electrical systems, including heating, cooling, ventilation, power and lighting.

FF&E

Furniture, fixtures and equipment associated with the interior.

Practical completion

A contractual milestone typically indicating the works are sufficiently complete for their intended use, subject to the contract terms.

Defects period

A contractual period for identifying and addressing defects in accordance with the agreed terms.

Long-lead item

A product or package whose design, manufacture or delivery time may affect the programme.

Landlord's fit-out guide

Building-specific requirements or procedures for proposed works, where provided.

Dilapidations

Lease-related obligations concerning the condition of premises and potential reinstatement or repair, subject to the lease and professional advice.

Change control

The process of recording and agreeing changes to the scope, price or programme.
