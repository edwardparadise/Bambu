FURNITURE / FF&E

Beautiful furniture. Properly coordinated.

Hover or select a stage to explore the decisions, coordination and deliverables.

01 / Work settings

Plan around use

Consider workstation types, meeting settings, storage and informal spaces.

What you get

Furniture brief and indicative schedule by workplace setting.

Workplace needs
Review headcount, hybrid working, meeting demand, storage and individual work patterns.
Setting mix
Compare task seating, sit-stand desks, collaboration, focus and breakout settings.
Inclusive design
Consider accessibility, circulation, adjustability and ergonomic requirements with users.

02 / Specification

Review specifications

Assess dimensions, durability, finishes, lead times and budget suitability.

What you get

Agreed furniture schedule, finish selections and procurement assumptions.

Product performance
Review ergonomics, durability, cleaning, warranty and relevant fire performance requirements.
Materials and samples
Compare finishes, upholstery, colours and compatibility with the wider interior scheme.
Commercial selection
Check supplier quotations, availability, lead times and alternatives against the budget.

03 / Interfaces

Coordinate the interfaces

Align furniture with power, data, lighting, circulation and installation access.

What you get

Coordinated furniture layout and interface schedule.

Spatial coordination
Check furniture dimensions, circulation, door swings and accessibility against the latest plans.
Power and technology
Coordinate floor boxes, desk power, cable management, AV and meeting-room equipment.
Installation access
Confirm lift sizes, delivery routes, assembly space and fixing requirements before ordering.

04 / Installation

Install and hand over

Agree delivery slots, assembly, protection and installation responsibilities alongside the construction programme.

What you get

Delivery plan, installed furniture schedule and care information.

Delivery planning
Agree supplier lead times, delivery slots, storage and readiness of finished areas.
Installation quality
Coordinate assembly, placement, protection, damage checks and snagging with suppliers.
User handover
Provide relevant adjustment guidance, care information, warranties and inventory details.
