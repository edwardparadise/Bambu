# BAMBU / Integrated artistic refinement
**Release:** 23 November 2026 — one coordinated deployment.

## Directives
Preserve the existing approved logo, original background source files and the delicate graphite pencil style. There must be no exact repeated hero/rotating sketch across audience, approach, delivery, material and contact pages. New page-specific drawings should remain light and only use pinpoints of colour. No additional personalised portraits.

## Artwork inventory / six per page
- Occupiers: original material conversation, three tenant-specific axonometric studies, one existing materials-review drawing, one live-project phasing line drawing.
- Landlords & agents: original site-walk drawing, the approved mid-CAD wireframe, three landlords-only perspective drawings, an independent floorplate/CAD scene.
- Workspace operators: original tour line drawing, five uniquely composed operator workplace drawings.
- Approach: original meeting pencil drawing, four unique stages of architectural coordination, a technical layer view.
- Materials: original graphite sample drawing, three unique material studies, two original sample-presentation compositions.
- Delivery network: original site linework, three delivery-specific interiors, original people-free completed scene, custom building interface linework.
- Contact: original warm interior drawing, three contact-only architectural studies, quiet meeting-room and reception scenes.

The currently published original binary source assets are retained in the repository; changing page assignments never deletes them. Every new SVG is actual separate vector content, not a CSS transform applied to an identical reused URL.

## Scene semantics
Occupiers technical workstreams receive five original, intentionally different drawings: structured cabling/comms rack with display, ventilation and lighting, landlord approvals, fire testing and handover, and independent certification. Operators receive a landlord/agent meeting, floorplate capacity, building services, programme and a handover study, plus four model-specific graphics. Delivery network principles receive six matching specialist/technical glyphs. The three BAMBU-in-practice cards have their own diagrams and real links to relevant services.

## Interaction/visual control
- In the six-scene header images, existing original image acts as fallback; load new artwork lazily where safe, respect reduced-motion preference.
- Add plainly worded slider cues and headings where the motion was not discoverable.
- Space design as complete information modules on laptop: 1366×768 baseline, plus 1440×900 and phone breakpoints. In the operator roadmap, left-hand intro and right-hand station/card form a single editorial composition; never crop copy just to force a panel height.
- On desktop, hover conveys purpose: backgrounds/diagrams respond, and BAMBU-in-practice cards link to useful detail pages rather than looking clickable without action.
- Architectural dots/annotations show CLIENT, DESIGN, APPROVALS, SERVICES etc. They are behind neither body copy nor key controls, and are hidden at narrower widths.
- Preserve the Tube lines' cyan, terracotta, bronze and green semantics; avoid bright small text on ivory. Keep the accessibility and keyboard states in the existing scroll controls.
