# BAMBU illustration art direction — approved 22 November 2026

## Status
Approved **visual language**, not blanket approval to replace or publish new illustrations.
Retain all currently published drawings until new replacements are individually checked against desktop/iPhone text legibility, subject relevance and image quality. Do not alter the BAMBU logo or any other branding files.

## Visual principles
- Use the existing BAMBU hand-drawn character sketches as the benchmark. Loose, delicate, light graphite pencil on warm ivory; visually editorial, not photorealistic or heavily brushed.
- Leave generous paper/negative space; distinguish the pencil sketch from copy, especially on smaller phones.
- Small, deliberate **brand colour pops**, not broad washes: BAMBU blue `#00B9EB`, soft terracotta, bronze `#B89B68`. Examples: a textile swatch, a chair, a detail of lighting, a line on a plan.
- Mix people-led conversations and site visits with people-free technical and spatial compositions.
- Figures may subtly reference the silhouettes or hairstyles of the three people in the supplied reference photos. **Do not show faces.** Clothing must remain professional (shirts, jackets, business dress, appropriate sitewear); no prominent jewellery, exposed cleavage or sexualised clothing.
- No reused artwork without a reason tied to the page context. No obvious repeated image immediately adjacent in a site's navigation journey.
- Target six different relevant scenes per page, illustrated as a coherent sequence rather than six unrelated wallpapers.
- Scene visible on initial page load; slow, gentle crossfades through scenes; never sacrifice legibility, accessibility, loading performance or reduced-motion support.
- Every scene needs its own mobile composition; avoid cropping people at joints or placing dark sketch detail directly behind primary headings and copy.
- No brand logos, fictional client logos or unverified claims within generated artwork.

## Production and QA checklist
- Preserve existing image as a verified fallback at load.
- Create truly separate scene artwork with contextually appropriate subject; do not substitute generic CAD wireframes or crops of multi-image moodboards.
- Test each 6-scene sequence at 390px, 430px, 768px, 1440px, and reduced-motion.
- No horizontal overflow, heading collision, inaccessible content, heavy CPU animation or dead space below footer.
- Optimise source files and appropriate responsive variants; avoid an upscale-only asset marketed as high-resolution original artwork.
- QA first; publish only scenes that meet the visual benchmark. The logo is explicitly out of scope.
