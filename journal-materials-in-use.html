Three questions to ask of a material sample

A finish earns its place through installation, cleaning and repair as well as appearance.

See it in the building

Take the sample to site and look at it beside a retained finish, in daylight and under the proposed lighting. Review a larger piece where grain or pattern varies.

Ask how it goes together

Check the substrate, joints, edge details and installation sequence with the specialist. An attractive sample alone tells you little about the finished junction at a door.

Plan the first repair

Agree how the facilities team will clean, repair and replace it. Keep the specification and supplier reference with the handover record. Record the product reference and maintenance instructions at handover.
