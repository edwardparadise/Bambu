THE BAMBU ROUTE

Your next workplace.
A connected journey.

Five stages from our first conversation to opening day. Follow where people, design, cost and construction work together.

STAGE 01 / 05
  1. 01 / UnderstandUnderstand

    Agree how your people work, what the building allows and what the business needs to achieve.

    Before drawing a layout, we need to understand what the workplace must achieve. We explore how your teams work, what is holding them back and how the business might change. Whether you are considering a move, renewing a lease or improving your existing office, we bring the operational, property and commercial requirements into one brief.

    This gives everyone a shared basis for comparing options, rather than making decisions around a layout or aesthetic alone.

    What you get

    A clear workplace brief, initial constraints and agreed priorities.

    People and workplace
    Review headcount, attendance, work settings, accessibility and future growth.
    Building and constraints
    Review available records, lease obligations, existing services and survey needs.
    Commercial priorities
    Agree budget expectations, target dates, decision owners and success criteria.
    Explore the detail

  2. 02 / PlanPlan

    Compare layouts, building suitability, budget, programme and approvals before committing to a space.

    Planning is about making informed commitments. We compare the suitability of buildings and layouts, considering the cost of making each option work, not simply the cost of the space itself. We review existing services, lease milestones, landlord requirements and the practical implications of moving or remaining in occupation.

    By testing budget, programme and technical constraints together, we can identify where compromises are necessary and which decisions need to be made early. The preferred option should have a credible route to delivery before significant expenditure is committed.

    What you get

    Options appraisal, outline cost plan and programme, with key risks recorded.

    Feasibility and test fits
    Compare layouts, capacity, circulation and technical suitability of shortlisted spaces.
    Cost and programme
    Develop indicative costs, contingency and a programme covering surveys, procurement and construction.
    Approvals and risks
    Identify landlord consent, applicable statutory approvals and specialist investigations.
    Explore the detail

  3. 03 / DesignDesign

    Bring the concept, materials, furniture and technical services together into a coordinated design.

    We develop the character and functionality of the workplace through layouts, materials, lighting, furniture and visual studies. The design is considered alongside the technical requirements of the building, so that the architecture, services and technology work together rather than being resolved independently.

    We review selections against cost and lead times, coordinate specialist input and establish what information is needed for approvals and procurement. You can understand the implications of each design decision before it becomes a construction commitment.

    What you get

    Agreed design package, coordinated specification and procurement priorities.

    Concept and materials
    Develop layouts, finishes, furniture, lighting and samples around the agreed brief.
    Technical integration
    Coordinate ventilation, power, data, AV, acoustics, fire systems and access control with specialists.
    Design release
    Track approvals, specification decisions, long-lead items and information required for construction.
    Explore the detail

  4. 04 / DeliverDeliver

    Manage procurement, construction, cost and quality, with a clear plan for opening day.

    Delivery starts well before the first trade arrives on site. We coordinate procurement, access, protection and specialist responsibilities around an agreed sequence of works. During construction, progress, quality and commercial performance are reviewed together, with changes and decisions recorded as they arise.

    We plan the final stages around occupation, coordinating furniture, technology, commissioning and inspections so the workplace is ready for its intended use, subject to the agreed completion requirements.

    What you get

    Progress and cost reporting, inspection records and a coordinated handover plan.

    Procurement and logistics
    Coordinate orders, access, deliveries, protection and trade sequencing.
    Cost and quality
    Track progress, valuations, changes, inspections and workmanship against agreed requirements.
    Completion readiness
    Coordinate snagging, specialist commissioning, furniture, technology and move-in arrangements.

  5. 05 / SupportSupport

    Coordinate handover, ongoing care and future adaptations as your business changes.

    Opening the office is the beginning of its working life, not the end of the relationship. We coordinate the relevant handover information, explain how to raise defects or operational questions and identify the specialists responsible for ongoing support.

    As your business evolves, we can help assess changes to layouts, furniture and technology, considering the existing investment before recommending further work. The aim is to keep the workplace useful, adaptable and aligned with the people who occupy it.

    What you get

    Handover information, agreed aftercare route and priorities for future changes.

    Handover and training
    Coordinate relevant manuals, certificates, warranties, user guidance and support contacts.
    Aftercare and maintenance
    Agree defect reporting, maintenance priorities and specialist support responsibilities.
    Future adaptation
    Review operational feedback, changing headcount and opportunities for targeted improvements.
