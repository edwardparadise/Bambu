WORKSTREAM / 0101

DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Fibre, networks and AV

We coordinate connectivity, cabling, Wi-Fi, meeting-room AV and access control, with lead times considered early.

WORKSTREAM / 0202

ENVIRONMENTAL SYSTEMS

Fresh air, cooling and lighting

We assess ventilation, cooling and lighting against the layout, occupancy and the way the space will be used.

WORKSTREAM / 0303

APPROVALS & COMPLIANCE

Licence to Alter and building control

We coordinate landlord consent, technical submissions and building control requirements before they delay the programme.

WORKSTREAM / 0404

SAFETY & COMMISSIONING

Fire safety and handover

We coordinate fire-safety input, testing, commissioning records and the information needed for a smooth handover.

WORKSTREAM / 0505

Project certification

Where relevant, we plan certification requirements such as BREEAM, SKA, WELL or WiredScore with the specialist assessors.
