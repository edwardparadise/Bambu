For occupiers / People first

Let's get to know how you work.

We'd love to hear about your project and get to know how your business operates. Ideally, we'd start by visiting your current office, meeting your team and understanding what needs to keep moving while any changes take place.

Whether you're considering a refurbishment or a relocation, we'll review the options together and coordinate the design, approvals, programme and delivery. We want to take the moving parts off your desk so you can focus on running your business.

The workplace brief / How you operate

Start with the space you need, and the building you have.

We look at where your teams naturally meet, where people need quiet focus and how meeting technology supports the working day. We plan the right balance of shared space, individual settings and practical facilities around the way you actually operate.

Whether you stay or move, that means decisions grounded in your people, your existing building and the demands of your business.

Behind the fit-out / Technical coordination

The details that keep your project moving.

From connectivity and building services to approvals and handover, we coordinate the technical workstreams that affect your people, programme and move-in.

01 / 05
SYSTEMS / 0101
TECHNICAL COORDINATION

DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Fibre, networks and AV

Check what fibre is available, advise on installation and lead times, and coordinate the provider, building access, comms room, cabling, Wi-Fi, meeting-room AV and access control.

SYSTEMS / 0202
TECHNICAL COORDINATION

ENVIRONMENTAL SYSTEMS

Fresh air, cooling and lighting

Review the existing fresh air supply, air conditioning and controls against the layout and proposed occupancy. Coordinate specialist checks and design for ventilation, thermal comfort and dimmable lighting.

SYSTEMS / 0303
TECHNICAL COORDINATION

APPROVALS & COMPLIANCE

Licence to Alter and building control

A Licence to Alter is the landlord's consent for relevant works. We coordinate the landlord submission, technical drawings and responses, and plan for applicable building control approvals, inspections and completion documentation.

SYSTEMS / 0404
TECHNICAL COORDINATION

SAFETY & COMMISSIONING

Fire safety and handover

Coordinate specialist input on fire safety plans, escape routes, alarms and alterations. Organise applicable test and commissioning records, certificates and operating information for handover.

SYSTEMS / 0505
TECHNICAL COORDINATION

SUSTAINABILITY & STANDARDS

Project certification

If BREEAM, SKA Rating, WELL or a digital connectivity target such as WiredScore is part of the brief, we help define the applicable scope and coordinate the evidence with the relevant assessors and building team.
