Tenants

Give people a reason to come in.

We design and build workplaces that earn the commute. From the route a visitor takes at reception to the room your team can book for a private call, we bring design, building services and technology together around the way you work.

Assessing a move or rethinking the office you already occupy? We test both against your brief, budget and programme before you commit.

Make the office work harder

Put touchdown points where teams naturally cross paths, give focused work a quieter address and make meeting technology easy to use. We plan the mix around what your people do together and what they need to do well on their own. That gives people reasons to spend time together, makes everyday work smoother and supports the experience that helps retain talent.

Reception and welcome

A clear arrival point, with access control at the right threshold and a place to meet visitors.

Front of house

Meeting rooms and a meet-and-greet area near reception, with connected AV and write-on surfaces.

Tea point and water

A shared destination for informal conversations, with a water point and space to pause.

Touchdown and focus

Short-stay workpoints along the main route, with private phone booths nearby for calls.

Team workspace

Sit–stand desks, write-on walls and dimmable lighting so people can work in different ways.

Back of house

Lockers, storage, equipment and a comms room, with servicing routes kept clear.

The technical work behind the fit-out

We bring the decisions that affect cost, approvals and move-in into the programme early, then coordinate the design and construction around them.

Fibre, networks and AV

Check what fibre is available, advise on installation and lead times, and coordinate the provider, building access, comms room, cabling, Wi-Fi, meeting-room AV and access control.

Fresh air, cooling and lighting

Review the existing fresh air supply, air conditioning and controls against the layout and proposed occupancy. Coordinate specialist checks and design for ventilation, thermal comfort and dimmable lighting.

Licence to Alter and building control

A Licence to Alter is the landlord’s consent for relevant works. We coordinate the landlord submission, technical drawings and responses, and plan for applicable building control approvals, inspections and completion documentation.

Fire safety and handover

Coordinate specialist input on fire safety plans, escape routes, alarms and alterations. Organise applicable test and commissioning records, certificates and operating information for handover.

Project certification

If BREEAM, SKA Rating, WELL or a digital connectivity target such as WiredScore is part of the brief, we help define the applicable scope and coordinate the evidence with the relevant assessors and building team.
