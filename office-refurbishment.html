How we can help

What the work involves

01

Start with the existing conditions

Review available records, existing finishes and building services. Identify surveys and specialist assessments that may be needed.

02

Set the priorities

Separate essential repairs, functional improvements and optional design enhancements to make investment decisions clearer.

03

Plan around occupancy

Consider access, noise, dust, working hours, temporary arrangements and how the team will operate during the works.

04

Deliver and hand over

Coordinate procurement, works, commissioning and the information required for the space to return to use.
