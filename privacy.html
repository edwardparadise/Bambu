Privacy & cookies

How BAMBU Interiors handles information shared through our website and how you can manage optional website analytics.

Updated 21 November 2026

Who we are

BAMBU Interiors is a London-focused commercial interior design and build business. For questions about personal information, email ed@bambuinteriors.co.uk.

Information you give us

If you contact us by email or send us a project enquiry, we receive the contact details and project information you choose to provide. We use this information to respond, assess possible work and manage any resulting client relationship. Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information in enquiries.

Optional analytics cookies

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find and use our website. This can include pages viewed, engagement, approximate location, device information and traffic source. Google's analytics service may set cookies such as _ga and related measurement cookies. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses or enquiry messages to Google Analytics.

Google Analytics is not loaded until you choose “Accept analytics”. Selecting “Reject optional” leaves the website usable without our optional Google Analytics tracking.

Change your cookie choice

Use the Cookie choices link at the bottom of any page to change your preference. Rejecting analytics after previously accepting stops further analytics loading after the page refreshes. Existing cookies can be removed through your browser's cookie settings.

Service providers and retention

We use appropriate technology and service providers to operate the website and respond to enquiries. Where Google Analytics is enabled, Google processes analytics data under its applicable terms and data policies. Data may be processed outside the UK with relevant safeguards. We retain business enquiries and correspondence only as needed for relevant operational, contractual or legal purposes.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact ed@bambuinteriors.co.uk. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office.

External sites

External websites linked from ours have their own privacy practices. Google's privacy policy explains how it handles information.
