THREE PROJECTS / ONE APPROACH

Specific work. Clearly delivered.

Our delivery history predates the BAMBU name. These projects were undertaken by the company formerly trading as INNA Design and Build. Each summary reflects the scope actually undertaken—not the complete fit-out of the wider building.

01 / WORKPLACE REFURBISHMENT

London

MSCI

24,000 SQ FT

Carpet replacement and raised-access-floor adjustments across the workplace, including relocation of underfloor power and data and management of furniture installation and relocations.

Site programme2 weeks

Delivered as specialist works through the main contractor. Furniture procurement was undertaken by others.

02 / TECHNICAL WORKPLACE RECONFIGURATION

3 Pancras Square / King's Cross, London

Havas Media

3,500 SQ FT

A design study and alterations to introduce additional meeting rooms in response to increased demand for online meetings.

The scope included layout changes, furniture relocations, underfloor power and data repositioning, lighting adjustments, modifications to sprinkler and fire alarm systems, access control alterations and AV installations across the meeting rooms.

Photography shows meeting-room accommodation within Havas' headquarters. The scope above relates to the company's later alteration works, not the original building-wide fit-out.

03 / MEETING ROOM & JOINERY

3 Pancras Square / King's Cross, London

Saga

6,000 SQ FT

Reconfiguration of the existing workplace to introduce an additional meeting room, including bespoke upholstered joinery providing extra seating for larger meetings.

Works included sound-absorbing fabric wall finishes, integrated AV, furniture relocations, underfloor power and data repositioning, lighting adjustments, fire alarm modifications and access control alterations.

Authentic project photography is being verified. No imagery from unrelated spaces is presented as work delivered for Saga.
