03 / WORKPLACE REFURBISHMENT

MSCI

London

24,000 SQ FT2-week site programme

The project

A focused workplace refurbishment involving removal and replacement of approximately 24,000 sq ft of carpet, together with raised access floor adjustments and the coordination of furniture installation and relocations.

Works also required floor boxes to be adjusted or relocated and associated underfloor electrical power and data cabling to be repositioned, allowing the finished workspace to operate around the new furniture layout.

01 / WORKPLACE RECONFIGURATION

Havas Media

3 Pancras Square · King’s Cross, London

3,500 SQ FTMeeting-space improvements

The project

A design study and associated alterations to introduce additional meeting spaces in response to the increasing demand for online meetings.

The scope included office layout reconfiguration, furniture relocations, underfloor power and data repositioning, lighting adjustments, sprinkler and fire alarm modifications, access control alterations and AV installations across all meeting rooms.

02 / BESPOKE MEETING SPACE

Saga

3 Pancras Square · King’s Cross, London

6,000 SQ FTMeeting-room reconfiguration

The project

Reconfiguration of the existing workplace to introduce an additional meeting room, including a bespoke upholstered joinery feature providing supplementary seating during larger meetings.

The work incorporated sound-absorbing fabric wall finishes, AV installations, furniture relocations, underfloor power and data repositioning, lighting adjustments, fire alarm modifications and access control alterations.
