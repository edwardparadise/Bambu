THE DELIVERY ROUTE / SIX CHECKPOINTS

From first look.
To first day.

Six practical checkpoints, grouped into three connected phases. A clearer way to understand what needs deciding, and when.

PHASE 01 / UNDERSTAND

Know the building.

01Test the building.Check the floorplate, services, lease and practical constraints before committing.Feasibility & test fits ↗ 02Set the budget.Agree scope, allowances and priorities, with the unknowns clearly identified.Project budgeting ↗
PHASE 02 / COORDINATE

Resolve the detail.

03Develop the design.Coordinate the layout, finishes, services and decisions that affect buildability.Workplace design ↗ 04Secure approvals.Prepare landlord submissions and allow for technical reviews and consents.Licence to Alter ↗
PHASE 03 / DELIVER

Bring it to life.

05Deliver the fit-out.Coordinate procurement, specialist trades, programme and quality on site.Fit-out delivery ↗ 06Handover and aftercare.Plan commissioning, documentation and the support needed after opening.Aftercare ↗

Every appointment is different. These checkpoints describe the thinking behind delivery, not a fixed contract or prescribed sequence.
