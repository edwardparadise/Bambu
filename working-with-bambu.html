What to expect

A practical relationship from day one.

Meet the people responsible for the work and agree how decisions, costs and changes will be recorded.

01 / The first conversation

We discuss the building, floor area, objectives, timing and what is already known. You do not need a finished brief.

02 / The early review

We identify missing information, constraints and the specialist input that may be needed before costs or dates can be relied on.

03 / The proposal

We set out the proposed scope, assumptions, responsibilities and next steps for agreement.

04 / Design and approvals

The design and landlord requirements are coordinated around the project brief and agreed appointment.

05 / Delivery planning

Procurement, access, programme, interfaces and quality expectations are considered before work begins.

06 / Handover

Completion, commissioning, outstanding items and relevant information are coordinated under the agreed contract.
