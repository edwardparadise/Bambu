ABOUT / BAMBU INTERIORS

A different perspective on getting it built.

BAMBU is a London-focused commercial interiors business for occupiers, landlords and flexible workspace operators. We believe a good idea is only as strong as the plan that brings it into the building.

01 / PRINCIPLE

A business built around decisions, not just drawings.

Commercial interiors are full of consequential decisions: whether to stay or move, what to retain, which products to trust, what the landlord will permit and how to deliver without unnecessary surprises. We bring those questions forward.

02 / PRACTICE

Experience without the theatre.

Our approach draws on experience in commercial project delivery, building constraints and stakeholder coordination. We are a developing business, not a vast anonymous team. Clients should know who is responsible for the work and how the wider delivery team is assembled.

03 / PRINCIPLE

Curious about what is new. Particular about what works.

We are interested in contemporary materials, lighting, flexible layouts and technology. But the installation method, evidence, maintenance and suitability for the building matter as much as the initial concept.

04 / PRACTICE

Built for a working relationship.

We value direct conversations, transparent assumptions and a sensible response when circumstances change. Our role is to connect the design ambition to the realities of a live construction project.
