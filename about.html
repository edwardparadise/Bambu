ABOUT BAMBU / WHAT MATTERS TO US

BAMBU is about better places to work.

We are an independent commercial interiors design and build business. We bring together thoughtful ideas, technical knowledge and construction expertise to create spaces that work as well as they look.

From the first building visit to the final detail on site, we believe good results come from understanding the brief, making clear decisions and taking responsibility for delivery.

Small by design. Big on what matters.

LONDON / DESIGN & BUILD THOUGHTFUL DESIGN / PRACTICAL DELIVERY

THE BAMBU ADVANTAGE / DESIGN MEETS DELIVERY

The creative attention of a studio.
The discipline of a builder.

We bring creative ideas and careful detailing together with the practical judgement, cost awareness and construction coordination needed to deliver them. That means design ambition informed by what a building will allow and what a project needs to achieve.

As a small-to-medium-sized business, BAMBU can stay close to the brief and bring in the right expertise without unnecessary layers. Whether it's a targeted refurbishment, a meeting-suite upgrade, a Cat A refurbishment or a complete Cat B fit-out, we welcome projects of different sizes and complexities, including those with demanding creative and technical requirements.

We take responsibility for the moving parts, from design and building services to landlord approvals, procurement and site delivery, so clients can focus on their business.

THE BAMBU WAY / OUR PRINCIPLES

A clear approach to the work.

We care about how a project feels for the people commissioning it, using it and building it. These principles shape the way we design, plan and deliver.

01 / LISTEN

Understand before proposing.

Start with the people, the business, the building and the practical challenges worth solving.

02 / DESIGN

Make every decision count.

Balance character and function with technical constraints, buildability, programme and budget.

03 / COORDINATE

Own the difficult details.

Bring designers, building services specialists, subcontractors and approvals into one coordinated plan.

04 / DELIVER

Take responsibility through handover.

Keep costs, decisions and site quality visible, with an accountable team from design to completion.

HOW WE THINK / HOW WE BUILD

Ask the difficult questions early.

A good workplace should be beautiful, practical and achievable. We bring commercial, design and construction thinking together early, so issues with services, landlord approvals, procurement and delivery are addressed while there is still room to make good decisions.
