OUR STORY / 2022 TO 2026

A different name.
A more personal direction.

There is a reason I chose to relaunch the business rather than simply carry on as before. I wanted to put the experience, relationships and lessons of the past fifteen years into a company that works the way I believe a contractor should.

  1. 2022

    Where the business began.

    The company was incorporated in 2022. We built experience delivering commercial workplace projects and developing the client relationships that would shape the next chapter.

  2. 2026

    The launch of BAMBU Interiors.

    In 2026, I rebranded the existing company as BAMBU Interiors. I wanted the name and identity to reflect a more direct, personal approach to commercial design and build, backed by work we'd already delivered.

A NOTE FROM OUR FOUNDER

I wanted to build the kind of business I'd want to appoint.

I've worked on commercial interiors projects for more than fifteen years. I've seen how much a good result depends on the people involved: who listens, who takes ownership and who is prepared to raise a difficult question before it becomes an expensive problem.

Big teams and impressive presentations have their place. But clients also deserve direct access to the people making decisions. They should know who is responsible for cost, programme, design coordination and what happens on site.

That's the thinking behind BAMBU. A business that's deliberately lean, with experienced leadership close to each project and trusted specialists brought together around the brief. We want to create places people are proud of, without losing sight of what those spaces need to do or what they cost to deliver.

It's a new name and a new direction, built around the parts of this industry I value most: relationships, good judgement and the satisfaction of delivering what we promised.

Ed ParadiseFounder · BAMBU Interiors

HOW WE WORK

Curiosity with a method.

One clear process. Practical decisions made at the right time.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Ask the difficult questions early.

What will the building allow? What does the lease require? What could affect the cost or programme?

02 / EXPLORE

Keep better options on the table.

Consider contemporary products, adaptable layouts and design ideas that genuinely improve the brief.

03 / VERIFY

Test the detail before committing.

Review product data, technical interfaces, installation methods, maintenance and specialist requirements.

04 / DELIVER

Make the design work on site.

Coordinate approvals, procurement, contractors, quality and handover against the agreed plan.
