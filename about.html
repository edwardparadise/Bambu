THE BAMBU ADVANTAGE / DESIGN MEETS DELIVERY

The creative attention of a studio.
The discipline of a builder.

We combine creative design, commercial judgement and technical coordination.

Our director’s experience spans landlord works, Cat A+ and Cat B fit-outs, and occupied workplaces, including work for Havas Media, Saga and MSCI.

Experience includes meeting-room fit-outs, bespoke joinery, acoustic finishes, furniture and flooring, alongside coordinated alterations to power, data, lighting, fire systems, access control and mechanical services.

We coordinate design, approvals, procurement and delivery, so clients can focus on their business.
