ABOUT BAMBU

Small by design.
Big on what matters.

We design and fit out workplaces for occupiers, landlords and serviced office operators. The people who shape the brief stay involved through design and delivery.

DESIGN-LEDCOMMERCIALLY GROUNDEDPERSONALLY INVOLVED

WHY WE STARTED / 001

Good spaces start with better conversations.

Too often, a promising idea travels through a chain of handovers before anyone asks what it will cost, how it will be built or whether the landlord will approve it.

BAMBU brings creative thinking and construction judgement together from day one. We ask the awkward questions early, make decisions openly and keep the people responsible for delivery in the conversation.

WHAT SETS THE TONE / 002

Considered ideas.
Clear decisions.

Four principles shape our decisions, from the first sketch to the last detail on site.

01 / THE MINDSET

Design with a point of view.

We want every space to feel considered, not copied. The best ideas come from understanding the people, the building and the opportunity.

02 / THE EXPERIENCE

Solid advice. Informed decisions.

Experience matters most when it prevents surprises. We bring cost, programme, building constraints and landlord approvals into the design conversation early.

03 / THE STRUCTURE

Direct by design.

You speak to the people doing the work. We bring in the right specialists for each brief, with clear responsibilities and no unnecessary layers between a question and an answer.

04 / THE AMBITION

Make the next project the proof.

We agree how the space needs to work, then follow those decisions through pricing, site delivery and handover.

How we think / 003

Curiosity with a method.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Ask the difficult questions early.

What will the building allow? What does the lease require? What could affect the cost or programme?

02 / EXPLORE

Keep better options on the table.

Consider contemporary products, adaptable layouts and design ideas that genuinely improve the brief.

03 / VERIFY

Test the detail before committing.

Review product data, technical interfaces, installation methods, maintenance and specialist requirements.

04 / DELIVER

Make the design work on site.

Coordinate approvals, procurement, contractors, quality and handover against the agreed plan.
