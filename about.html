ABOUT / THE BAMBU STORY

New thinking.
Experienced foundations.

BAMBU is an independent London interiors business. Clients work directly with the people designing, pricing and delivering their space.

WHY WE STARTED / 001

There is room to do things differently.

A layout can pass through several hands before anyone prices it. By then, a services constraint or long lead time can force a redesign.

Ed started BAMBU to bring design and delivery into the same conversation from the first site visit.

01 / THE MINDSET

Curious by nature.

We enjoy the creative side of the job: imagining spaces, exploring materials and finding useful ways to make workplaces distinctive.

02 / THE EXPERIENCE

Grounded in delivery.

BAMBU is a new business, but its approach draws on experienced commercial project leadership. We consider the building, approvals, cost, programme and construction interfaces alongside the design.

03 / THE STRUCTURE

Lean on purpose.

We keep communication direct and assemble specialist support around the needs of each project. Roles, appointments and responsibilities are agreed clearly rather than hidden behind a large organisational chart.

04 / THE AMBITION

Build a reputation worth keeping.

Our ambition is measured in the quality of the work and the relationships that follow it. We want clients to return because the process was as considered as the result.

THE EXPERIENCE BEHIND BAMBU / 002

A new business.
Not a new discipline.

BAMBU was founded by Ed Paradise, a commercial interiors project director. His experience informs how we approach briefs, coordinate design decisions and plan delivery. Previous project experience remains distinct from work contracted by BAMBU.
