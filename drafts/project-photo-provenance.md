## Ground rules
The company now trading as **BAMBU Interiors** is a rebrand of INNA Design and Build. Project work undertaken by that business before the rebrand can be described as the company's work. Do not imply we designed or built the entire base building or an unrelated photographed fit-out. A Google Images result is not a photograph licence.
