DELIVERY / CONTROL

One programme. Clear accountability.

Hover or select a stage to explore the decisions, coordination and deliverables.

01 / Programme

Plan the work

Set out milestones, responsibilities, procurement requirements and construction interfaces.

What you get

Coordinated baseline programme, responsibility matrix and package schedule.

Package planning
Define trade scopes, exclusions, dependencies and the responsible contractor for each interface.
Programme controls
Map design release, procurement, site access and inspection milestones against the target completion date.
Responsibilities
Agree reporting lines, decision owners and the applicable contractual and statutory dutyholder appointments.

02 / Coordination

Coordinate contractors

Coordinate trades, suppliers, site interfaces and the wider project team.

What you get

Interface action register, coordinated short-term programme and inspection plan.

Trade interfaces
Resolve ceiling, partition, floor, joinery and services junctions before installation.
Site coordination
Review access, working hours, deliveries, protection and daily sequencing with contractors.
Quality and safety interfaces
Track inspection requirements, outstanding design information and relevant safety arrangements with dutyholders.

03 / Change control

Track change

Record scope changes, price implications and programme impacts before the team acts on revised instructions.

What you get

Change register with cost, programme and approval status.

Scope and instruction
Record proposed changes and identify who has authority to approve them under the contract.
Cost and timing
Obtain quotations and assess effects on procurement, sequence, contingency and completion.
Decision tracking
Maintain an instruction and variation register, including pending decisions and forecast exposure.

04 / Completion

Close out properly

Coordinate inspections, outstanding works and relevant handover information.

What you get

Completion tracker, handover information and commercial close-out status.

Completion inspections
Coordinate trade inspections, snagging, corrective work and client walkthroughs.
Technical handover
Track relevant commissioning records, certificates, manuals and warranties from appointed specialists.
Commercial close-out
Review outstanding valuations, final account information and unresolved contractual matters.
