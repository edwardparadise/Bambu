The essentials

What we look at.

01 / Define the outcome

Agree the business objectives, headcount, working patterns, essential rooms and non-negotiable constraints.

02 / Assess the space

Check layout opportunities, building services, power, ventilation, access, loading, fire strategy and connectivity with the appropriate specialists.

03 / Agree the commercial parameters

Identify the budget range, target dates, procurement approach, contingency assumptions and what is included or excluded.

04 / Understand the approvals

Identify landlord consent, building management rules, applicable statutory requirements and the parties responsible for each.

05 / Coordinate the design

Develop the spatial plan, finishes, furniture, AV, technology and building services as one joined-up brief.

06 / Plan delivery and handover

Agree access, working hours, long-lead items, testing, commissioning, defects and the information needed by the occupying team.

Useful to know

Questions worth asking.

When should the fit-out contractor become involved?

Early enough to test buildability, budgets and programme before design decisions are locked in.

Is this a complete compliance checklist?

No. Every building and project differs. Specialist designers, advisers and the relevant authorities determine applicable requirements.
