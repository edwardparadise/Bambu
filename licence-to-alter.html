The essentials

What we look at.

Identify the requirements

Review available landlord fit-out guides, lease-related requirements supplied by the project team and building management procedures.

Coordinate the information

Bring together drawings, specifications, building services proposals and specialist input required for the submission.

Manage the interfaces

Work with the landlord's surveyor, managing agent, design team and relevant specialists to respond to technical comments.

Plan for the programme

Allow for review periods, design revisions, procurement and any separate statutory approvals rather than treating consent as an afterthought.

Useful to know

Questions worth asking.

Does landlord consent replace building control approval?

No. Landlord consent and statutory requirements are separate processes. The approvals required depend on the works and the building.

Can you guarantee approval?

No. Approval is determined by the relevant landlord and authorities. We can coordinate the information and process, not promise their decisions.

PROJECT NOTE

Approval is a process, not a promise

Landlord consent depends on the lease, building requirements and the landlord's decision. Early coordination and complete technical information help reduce avoidable delays, but approval cannot be guaranteed.
