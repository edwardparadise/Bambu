FROM LEAD-IN TO OPENING DAY

The whole fit-out, thoughtfully connected.

Plan procurement, quality checks and commissioning from the outset, with clear decisions and change control.

  1. 01

    Agree the lead-in

    Agree brief, scope, budget and opening date. Allow for surveys, design, landlord approvals and access before fixing the site start.

  2. 02

    Order the long-lead items early

    Identify long-lead joinery, glazing, furniture, lighting and equipment. Agree specifications, samples and manufacturing slots before placing orders.

  3. 03

    Set the site up properly

    Agree access, welfare, protection, storage, deliveries and working hours. Brief the team and keep neighbouring occupiers informed.

  4. 04

    Build in a coordinated sequence

    Sequence specialist trades, inspect interfaces and concealed works, and report progress and decisions.

  5. 05

    Review progress, cost and quality together

    Review progress, valuations, variations, forecast cost and programme at agreed intervals.

  6. 06

    Check and snag progressively

    Inspect work progressively, record and close out snags, protect completed areas and arrange client walkthroughs.

  7. 07

    Install the finishing systems and furniture

    Coordinate furniture, AV, IT, access control, security, connections and user testing with finishes and the move-in plan.

  8. 08

    Commission, hand over and open

    Complete agreed commissioning, integrated checks, user training, manuals, warranties and completion records before handover.
