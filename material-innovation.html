Materials & specification

Finishes with a purpose.
A palette shaped around you.

Materials are selected around the way the workplace will be used, the design ambition and the budget. We compare appearance, comfort, durability, cleaning, repair and installation before bringing the finishes together as one coordinated palette.

Samples and product specification sheets make those choices easier to discuss. We can refine the proposed finishes to support your project’s assessment and accreditation aspirations, working with the relevant assessor and checking the evidence behind each selection.

Discuss a specification

From your brief to a considered specification.

  1. 01

    Set the priorities

    Agree the character of the space, practical performance, budget and assessment aspirations. Review what can be retained or reused before specifying something new.

  2. 02

    Explore samples and data

    Compare proposed products using physical samples and specification sheets. Review colour, texture, cleaning, durability, lead times and compatibility with the intended use.

  3. 03

    Refine and coordinate

    Adjust the palette with you, check the information needed for the agreed assessment route, and coordinate the chosen finishes with the technical design and procurement programme.

Project assessment

Which standard fits the brief?

Agree your assessment aspirations with the client, landlord and relevant assessor early. We can adjust the proposed finishes and gather product evidence around those agreed priorities. These independent schemes assess individual projects; any rating depends on formal assessment.

SKArating
Benchmarks a fit-out against good practice. An accredited assessor identifies the measures that apply to the work and verifies the evidence for certification.
BREEAM
Assesses the environmental performance of refurbishment and fit-out work. A licensed assessor can confirm the route for a tenant floor, landlord works or a wider refurbishment.
WELL
Focuses on how the building supports health and wellbeing. Performance verification includes on-site testing of conditions such as air quality, light and sound.
Fitwel
Uses a scorecard of design and operational measures that support health. The appropriate scorecard depends on the building and the space covered by the project.
WiredScore
Assesses digital connectivity in office buildings, including the internet service options and the infrastructure that supports a reliable connection. If the brief calls for a rating, agree the target with the landlord and confirm what the building can support.
