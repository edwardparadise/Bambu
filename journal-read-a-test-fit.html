How to read a test fit before signing a lease

Look beyond the desk count. Trace three journeys through the proposed workplace.

Walk in as a visitor

Follow the route from the lift to reception and the meeting room. Check what a visitor can see and whether anyone has to interrupt the working floor to show them through.

Try a busy Tuesday

Picture colleagues arriving together, calls starting and a meeting breaking for lunch. A drawing can show enough chairs while leaving too little space to pull them out or pass behind them.

Ask what has been checked

A test fit is an early spatial study, not a fully verified design. Confirm measured dimensions, services information and specialist checks before relying on it. Keep those questions with the plan when comparing buildings.
