# BAMBU visual direction — November 2026

The homepage and current illustrated Materials & Specification introduction are the editorial benchmarks. Do not replace artwork simply to make a page look new.

## Non-negotiable principles
- The first architectural image is visible on load, with an eager first frame.
- Headlines and copy have a soft local veil; outside and lower edges retain more drawing detail.
- Use site-specific palettes: terracotta for people and materials, bronze for approvals and finance, cyan for technical systems, and slate for construction and aftercare.
- The deep teal-black masthead, bronze rules, cyan logo, and square/circle geometry remain consistent with the mobile navigation.
- All background art is decorative with empty alt; core content remains selectable HTML text.
- Keep the five bespoke technical drawings conceptual, not construction documents or verified calculations.
- Retain the editorial 100svh openings, unified exterior gutters, and one wayfinding cue.
- Footer must be the physical end of every document, including short pages, at mobile and desktop widths.

## Bespoke drawings
- Construction Management: services coordination, trade interfaces and structure.
- AV & Technology: meeting-room screens, data and connectivity.
- Licence to Alter: approved changes, partitions and door swing.
- Project Budgeting: cost plan, scope packages and a test-fit diagram.
- Project Programme: overlapping design, commercial, procurement and construction streams.

## Existing artwork preserved
- Homepage, About BAMBU, Our Approach, Materials & Specification, Furniture & FF&E, and Journal.
- Occupiers, Landlords, Workspace Operators, Delivery Network, Contact, Maintenance and other service pages use modest contrast and positioning adjustments, not wholesale replacement.
- Guide/checklist pages remain deliberately text-led.
